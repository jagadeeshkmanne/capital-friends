Insurance tracker

At claim time the first question is "which policy, with whom, and who is the nominee?". The Insurance page answers it for every member: term and health policies, sum assured, premium and renewal date, the nominee on each, and a coverage check that tells you in red when the family is under-insured.

Adding a policy

  1. Insurance → Add Policy. Type (term life, health, endowment, ULIP, vehicle, other), the insurer and a policy name the family will recognise.
  2. Whose life or health it covers (the insured member) and the nominee. The nominee field is on every policy on purpose.
  3. Sum assured, premium and frequency; the policy number is optional (even the last few digits make a claim faster).
  4. Add. Renewal reminders are created from the frequency; the premium date reaches you by email before it is due.

Coverage adequacy

The page adds up the family's cover and compares it with two common guidelines:

CoverGuidelineShown as
Life (term) cover10–15× the earner's annual incomeCovered, or the gap in rupees
Health cover₹50 L to ₹1 Cr family floater, considering medical inflationCovered, or Not covered in red

The same two checks drive the financial health check and the Action required cards on the dashboard: add a health policy and the "No health insurance" card goes away by itself.

What the family sees

In the daily email each member's section lists their policies with insurer, type, cover, premium and nominee. If something happens, the family opens one email and knows which company to call and who the nominee is, instead of searching cupboards for policy documents.

Three things worth checking today

  • Every policy has a nominee, and the nominee knows.
  • Term cover is in the 10–15× income range while there are dependants and loans.
  • The health floater covers the whole family and has kept up with medical inflation.
Guidelines are planning rules of thumb. Read each policy's terms and talk to a qualified adviser for your own case.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app sell insurance?

No. It records the policies you have and checks the cover against common guidelines. It earns nothing from any insurer.

Why does it ask for a nominee on every policy?

Because at claim time that is the very first question, and a policy with the wrong or missing nominee is the most common reason claims stall.

How much term cover does it recommend?

It uses the common guideline of 10–15 times annual income and shows the gap if the family is below it. It is a benchmark, not advice.

Will it remind me before the premium is due?

Yes. Renewal reminders are created from the premium frequency and arrive by email before the date.

Can I track my parents' policies?

Yes, any member can be the insured on a policy, and each member's policies appear in their own section of the email.
