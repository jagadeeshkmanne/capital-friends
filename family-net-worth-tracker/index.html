Family net worth tracker

One dashboard for the whole family: net worth, total assets, the loans, the split across every asset type, the funds below their high, the portfolios that need a rebalance, the best performers, and all goals with their progress. Every number belongs to a person and comes from the statements you imported.

Why net worth, and why the family's

Income says what you earn; net worth says what you keep. Tracking it for the family, not just yourself, is what makes the number honest: your spouse's funds, the parents' FDs, the kids' education portfolios and the home loan all belong in it. And it is the number your family will need on the worst day: where everything is, and what it is worth.

What it adds up

Mutual funds

Every family member's funds from CAMS statements, with money-in, XIRR and CAGR.

Stocks

Holdings from broker Excel or CDSL/NSDL statements, per demat account.

Other investments

FDs, PPF, EPF, NPS, bonds, gold and silver by weight at today's rate, property.

Loans

Home, car and personal loans with EMI and months left; net worth is assets minus loans.

Insurance

Not an asset, but listed with cover and nominees so the family knows.

Bank and investment accounts

Which accounts exist and which platform is mapped to which bank.

The dashboard, section by section

  1. Net worth: assets, loans and the net figure, with a bar of the split (equity, debt, real estate, gold, commodities).
  2. Asset allocation: a donut with every asset type and its share; the first time most families see that 73% of their money is in one place.
  3. Action required: the open items from the financial health check, until each is fixed.
  4. Below high: funds 5% or 10% below their own all-time high, with the percentage.
  5. Rebalance needed: portfolios whose funds have drifted from their targets, with the amounts.
  6. Fund and portfolio performance: top gainers by rupee and percentage, best portfolios by return.
  7. Financial goals: every goal with have-today, the target, years left and on-track status.

Kept current without you

NAVs, stock prices and the gold rate refresh daily through the scheduled update that runs in your own Google account; the net worth on the dashboard and in the morning email is today's. Only things without a market price (an FD, EPF, the house) are updated when you choose.

Private by design

There is no bank login and no server database. All of this lives in one Google Sheet in your Drive; switch on the mask to hide names and numbers on screen when you share it. Read how your data is stored.

Frequently asked questions

Is the family net worth tracker free?

Yes, free and open source for any family, with no ads. Donations are voluntary.

Does it need bank logins?

No. Funds come from a CAMS statement, stocks from a broker file, and everything else you enter once. Bank accounts are a list.

Can I track my parents' money too?

Yes. Add them as members, import their CAMS statements, add their FDs and policies. Each has their own section in the email.

How is gold valued?

By weight and purity at the live rate, refreshed daily.

Does the net worth include the house and the loan?

Yes: the property at the value you enter, and the loan linked to it under liabilities. Net worth is assets minus loans.

What is the net worth calculator?

A quick no-sign-in version for a rough number: the net worth calculator. The app does it with your real statements every day.
