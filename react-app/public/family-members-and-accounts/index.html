Family members, bank accounts and investment accounts

In Capital Friends everything belongs to a person. Before any numbers you add the family: you, your spouse, parents, kids. Then the accounts: which banks, which investment platforms, and which bank each platform pays into. It takes ten minutes and it is the list your family would otherwise spend months rebuilding.

Why start with people, not money

A mutual fund is not just a fund; it is your mother's fund, in her Groww account, linked to her Axis bank account, with your father as nominee. When the app knows the people first, every later entry (a fund, a policy, a loan) is tied to the right person, the family net worth splits correctly, and the daily email can say where everything is for each member.

Family members

  1. Open Family Members → Add Member. Name, relationship (Self, Spouse, Son, Daughter, Father, Mother…), date of birth and email are the main fields.
  2. PAN, Aadhaar and mobile are optional. If something happens to you, these are the first things a bank or AMC asks your family for, so adding them saves a search; they are stored only in your sheet and masked on screen.
  3. Tick Include in email reports for the members who should receive the daily family email.
  4. Repeat for everyone. Kids are added as members too; their minor mutual fund accounts become their own portfolios when you import your CAMS statement.

Names on screen are hidden the moment you switch on the mask (the eye icon in the header), which is what you will see in our demo videos. Each member form also takes custom fields (occupation, blood group, employer, anything the family should know), and the Viewing as switch under your profile picture filters every page to one member or shows everyone together.

Bank accounts

This is a list, not a connection. For each account you record the member, the bank and the type (savings, current, FD, NRE/NRO). Account number, IFSC and branch are optional; even the last four digits are enough for the family to identify it. No login, no balance fetching: the point is that the family knows the account exists and whose it is.

Investment accounts

An investment account is where you actually invest from: a Zerodha or Groww account, a demat account, an AMC direct account, or a distributor (ARN). For each one you record the member, the platform, the account type (Demat + Trading, Mutual Fund, Direct AMC, Broker) and, the useful part, the bank account it is mapped to. Registered email and client ID are optional.

When your CAMS statement is imported, the app recognises the accounts in it and creates a portfolio per account, so the investment-account list and the fund portfolios line up by themselves.

What the family sees

Every account appears on its member's section of the daily email: bank name, type, the platform, the linked bank. Nothing a thief could use (no full account numbers unless you chose to add them), everything a spouse would need to make a call to the right bank or broker.

What comes next

With members and accounts in place, the rest of the app is just adding what each person holds: import mutual funds from a CAMS statement, stocks from your broker, FDs, EPF, gold and property, and insurance policies with nominees.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to enter account numbers?

No. The account number, IFSC and branch are optional. Many families enter only the last four digits so the account can be identified without exposing it.

Why add PAN and Aadhaar?

When your family has to claim an investment or insurance, PAN and Aadhaar of the holder are the first things they are asked for. It is your call; the fields are optional, saved only in your own sheet, and masked on screen and in the email.

Can I add my parents and in-laws?

Yes. Any relationship can be added, and each member can have their own bank accounts, investment accounts, funds, policies and goals.

Does the app connect to my bank?

Never. Bank accounts are a list so that the family knows which accounts exist. Balances are not fetched.

What is "mapped bank account" on an investment account?

The bank account your Zerodha, Groww or distributor account pays into and is funded from. Recording it tells the family where redemptions will land.
