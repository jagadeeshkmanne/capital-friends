What Capital Friends does differently

The whole family, everything

Every member's mutual funds and stocks, plus FDs, PPF, EPF, NPS, bonds, gold and silver by weight, property, insurance and loans, in one family net worth.

Real numbers from your statement

Import a CAMS + KFintech CAS PDF and every purchase, SIP, switch and redemption comes in with its date. Switches are not counted as new money, so money-in, XIRR and CAGR are right.

Your data in your Drive

No bank or broker password and no server database. The app works on one Google Sheet in your own Drive; download it as Excel any time.

Planning, not just tracking

Target allocation and rebalancing three ways, goals with a glide path, the three-bucket retirement strategy and a financial health check.

An email for the family

A daily or monthly email to the people you choose, with where everything is.

Free and open source

No ads and no paid tier. The code is on GitHub.

Where Capital Friends falls short

  • No account linking. Holdings come from statements and files you import, or what you type in. A fresh CAS each month keeps it current; NAVs and prices update daily on their own.
  • No investing. You buy and sell where you do today and record it in the app.
  • A web app, not a native one. It works in the phone browser; there is no Play Store or App Store app.
  • India only. Rupees, AMFI NAVs and NSE prices.
  • No research or ratings. It shows facts about your own money, not which fund to buy.

Using it alongside your investing app

You do not have to switch. Keep investing wherever you do today. One CAMS + KFintech CAS import per family member brings their mutual funds into Capital Friends, next to the FDs, EPF, gold, insurance and loans; the CAS import guide shows the steps.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best free portfolio tracker in India?

It depends on the job. To invest and track in one app, INDmoney and Kuvera are popular. For fund research with a tracker, Value Research Online. For a free tracker for the whole family that keeps the data in your own Google Drive and also covers FDs, EPF, gold, insurance, loans, goals and retirement buckets, Capital Friends.

Is Capital Friends an alternative to INDmoney, Kuvera or Value Research?

For tracking and planning, yes. For investing or fund research, no: Capital Friends does not sell or recommend funds. Many families use it alongside the app they invest with.

Does Capital Friends need my bank or broker login?

No. It never asks for passwords and does not link accounts. Holdings come from your CAMS + KFintech CAS, a broker Excel or CDSL/NSDL statement, or what you type in.

Can I track my whole family in one place?

Yes. Add each member, import each member's CAS, and see every portfolio on its own and the family total together, with goals and insurance for each person.

Why is Capital Friends free?

It was built by an investor for his own family and shared openly. There are no ads and no paid tier; you can support it with a voluntary donation.
