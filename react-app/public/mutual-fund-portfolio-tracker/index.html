Mutual Fund Portfolio Tracker

Track every mutual fund of every family member in one place, from one CAMS statement. Real money-in, XIRR and CAGR, allocation targets, rebalancing suggestions and funds below their high, with the data kept in a Google Sheet in your own Drive.

What makes it different from Groww, Kuvera or a spreadsheet

Real money-in, not holdings cost

When you switch funds, most apps count the switch as new investment and inflate "invested". Capital Friends shows the money that actually left your bank, the holdings cost, and the profit, realised and unrealised, separately.

The whole family

Your funds, your spouse's, your parents' and the kids' minor accounts, each as its own portfolio and together as one number. Import each person's CAS statement the same way.

XIRR and CAGR for everything

For every fund, every portfolio and the family total, from the exact date of every purchase, SIP, switch and redemption.

Below its high

Every fund is compared with its own all-time-high NAV every day. You see which of your funds is 5% or 10% below its peak, instead of reacting to a Nifty headline. Information, not advice.

Rebalancing you can act on

Set a target allocation per fund. The app shows the drift and three ways to fix it: adjust SIP amounts, add a lump sum to what is short, or buy and sell. Funds that no longer accept fresh money can be marked and skipped.

Your data, your Drive

No bank login, no server database. One Google Sheet in your Drive holds everything; delete it and the app has nothing.

How to import your mutual funds

  1. On the CAMS website choose a Detailed consolidated account statement, pick the start date (go back to when you first invested) and set a password. It arrives by email in a few minutes, free.
  2. Open Capital Friends, go to Mutual Funds, click Import from CAMS statement, choose the PDF and type the password.
  3. The app reads every fund and every transaction, creates one portfolio per account (including minor accounts for your kids), and asks you to confirm before importing. You can undo an import any time.

What you see after the import

A Mutual Funds page with money-in, current value, unrealised and realised profit, XIRR, CAGR and monthly SIP; the asset-class split; the investment journey since your first purchase; and every portfolio with its own return. Open a portfolio for the holdings table with units, NAV, average NAV, distance from the high, and current versus target allocation. Open a fund for its journey and every transaction with its own profit.

Fund breakdown: large, mid, small, global

Classify each fund once (asset class, market cap, geography, copied from Morningstar) and the app shows how much of the family's money is large cap, mid cap, small cap, debt, gold and outside India. Goals use this split to decide which funds are safe money and which are growth money.

Get XIRR for every fund, automatically

Stop typing cashflows. Import your CAMS statement into Capital Friends and get XIRR, CAGR, real money-in and profit for every fund, every portfolio and the whole family. Free and open source.

Frequently asked questions

Is the mutual fund tracker free?

Yes, free with no ads and open source. You can support the project with a voluntary donation.

Which statements can I import?

The detailed CAMS + KFintech consolidated account statement (CAS) PDF, which covers all AMCs. One statement per PAN; import one for each family member.

Does it track SIPs automatically?

Yes. Every SIP instalment is a transaction in the CAS, so the app knows your running SIPs, their amounts and dates, and sends reminders before the next date.

How are NAVs updated?

Daily from AMFI data through a scheduled script that runs in your own Google account, so values and the below-high alerts are current even when you do not open the app.

Can I use it as an Excel or Google Sheets portfolio tracker?

Your data already lives in a Google Sheet in your Drive. Open it any time, or download it as Excel.
