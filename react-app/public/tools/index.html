Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes, free with no login and no ads. They run in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

How accurate are they?

They use standard formulas (monthly-compounded SIP, compound growth, XIRR by bisection). Results depend on the return and inflation you assume.

Can I use the numbers for my own portfolio?

Yes: Capital Friends, the free app behind these pages, applies the same maths to your real mutual funds, stocks, EPF and FDs for the whole family.
