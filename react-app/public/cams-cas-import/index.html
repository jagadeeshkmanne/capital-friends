Import Your CAMS CAS Statement

The consolidated account statement from CAMS lists every mutual fund transaction you have ever made, across all AMCs. Import it once and your whole mutual fund history is in the tracker, with no typing.

Step 1: get the detailed CAS from CAMS

  1. Go to the CAMS statements page and choose the consolidated account statement, Detailed type (the summary version has no transactions).
  2. Choose the period. Pick a start date before your first investment; many people simply choose the earliest year offered so nothing is missed.
  3. Enter the email registered with your folios and set a password for the PDF. The statement arrives by email in a few minutes. It is free.

Step 2: import it

  1. On the CAMS website choose a Detailed consolidated account statement, pick the start date (go back to when you first invested) and set a password. It arrives by email in a few minutes, free.
  2. Open Capital Friends, go to Mutual Funds, click Import from CAMS statement, choose the PDF and type the password.
  3. The app reads every fund and every transaction, creates one portfolio per account (including minor accounts for your kids), and asks you to confirm before importing. You can undo an import any time.

What the import gives you

  • Every purchase, SIP, switch in/out, STP and redemption with its real date and NAV.
  • Real money-in (cash from your bank) separate from holdings cost, so switches do not inflate the invested amount.
  • XIRR and CAGR for each fund, each portfolio and the family.
  • Separate portfolios for each account in the statement, including kids' minor accounts; fully-sold accounts are skipped.
  • Past imports listed with an undo button.

Importing the whole family

A CAS is per PAN. Request one for your spouse, your parents and anyone else you track, and import each the same way. Minor accounts come inside the guardian's statement and get their own portfolios.

Frequently asked questions

CAMS or KFintech: which statement do I need?

The CAMS "CAS" covers both CAMS- and KFintech-serviced AMCs, so one statement is enough. Choose the detailed version with transactions.

What is the password for the CAS PDF?

The one you set while requesting it on the CAMS site. The tracker needs it to open the PDF; it is used only on your device and never stored.

Will importing twice duplicate transactions?

No. Import the same statement again or a newer one and existing transactions are matched, not doubled. You can also undo any import.

Does it work with NSDL or CDSL CAS?

Depository statements are for demat holdings. For stocks, Capital Friends imports the broker's holdings Excel or a CDSL/NSDL statement; for mutual funds use the CAMS CAS.
