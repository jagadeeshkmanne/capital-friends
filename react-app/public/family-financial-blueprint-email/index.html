The family financial blueprint: one email every morning

In most families all of the money sits in one person's head. This is the feature Capital Friends was built for: every morning, from your own Gmail, your family receives one email with every account, every fund, every policy and its nominee, every loan and every goal. If something happens to you, they open one email and know where everything is.

Why an email, and why daily

After losing someone, families search: where are the investments, which bank, who do we call. A PAN traces some of it, months later; much of it is never found. An email is the one thing everyone knows how to open, it is in their inbox even if the app is forgotten, and sending it daily means it is never out of date. Nobody has to remember to share anything.

Setting it up

  1. Add your family members and tick Include in email reports for those who should receive it. Each needs an email address.
  2. Switch on Daily updates in Settings → Data & Sync, so the email can go out from a schedule in your own Google account.
  3. In Settings → Email reports, choose daily or monthly and the send time, pick the recipients and save. Send Report Now sends a test immediately.
  4. Check the inbox. The subject is <family> Family – Financial Summary | net worth – date; the email is self-contained, with every section above.

Reminders inside it

The app creates reminders from what it already knows (SIP dates from your CAS, premium dates from policies, FD maturities) and lets you add your own: loan EMIs, an investment review, anything custom. They are listed in the email before they fall due, so a missed SIP or a lapsed policy stops being a surprise.

Privacy of the email

  • It is sent from your Gmail account by a script running in your Google account; it never passes through a Capital Friends server.
  • PAN, Aadhaar and account numbers are masked. Members see the family picture, not identity documents.
  • You choose who receives it; a spouse and one adult child is a common choice.

The task we give everyone

Ask your spouse or your parents tonight: where do we invest, which insurance do we have, who is the nominee? If they cannot answer, this email is the fix, and it takes an hour to set up from an empty Drive. The demo video shows the whole thing.

Frequently asked questions

Who sends the email?

Your own Gmail account, through a scheduled script in your Google account. Capital Friends has no server; nothing is sent through us.

Can I choose who gets it?

Yes. Tick Include in email reports on each member, and pick the recipients again in Settings → Email reports. You can send it to yourself only.

Daily or monthly?

Either, at a time you choose. Daily keeps it current; monthly keeps the inbox quiet. The alerts (funds below their high, rebalance, goal steps) also reach the recipients.

Are PAN and account numbers in the email?

They are masked. The email tells the family which bank and which platform; it is not a document for identity theft.

What if I stop using the app?

Switch off daily updates in Settings and delete the sheet from your Drive. The emails stop and no data remains.
