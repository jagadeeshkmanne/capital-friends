Frequently asked questions

Is Capital Friends free?

Yes. It is open source and free for any family. You sign in with Google and your data is saved in one Google Sheet in your own Drive.

Do I need to type in every fund?

No. Import one CAMS statement per family member and every fund and transaction comes in; the guide on CAMS import shows the steps.

Is this investment advice?

No. The app and these guides show facts about your own money; what you do with them is your decision.
