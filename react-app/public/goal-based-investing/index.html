Goal-based Investing, With Your Real Portfolio

Most goal calculators stop at a number. Capital Friends puts your goals on a timeline, counts the money you already have against them, shows the gap, and tells you each year how much to move from equity to safe so the money is there on the date.

How goals work in the app

  1. Add a goal. Retirement, child education, emergency fund, house, car, travel, wedding or custom. For child education the date is set from the child's age and the course; for retirement from your age and retirement age.
  2. See the number. Today's cost inflated to the goal year, the SIP required or the lumpsum today, and a suggested equity/debt split for that horizon.
  3. Money for goals. Tick which investments are for goals. You do not link funds to goals one by one: the app shares the pool, giving the emergency fund and near goals the safe money and far goals the equity. Kids' own portfolios and the house can be left out.
  4. Follow the road. Each goal shows have-today versus need-today, on track or not, the funds counted for it, and the year-by-year plan to shift from equity to safe.

Why a glide path matters

A ₹47 lakh college fee due in 2033 should not be 100% in equity in 2032. The app starts shifting five years out, a little each year and only in good markets, so a bad year right before the date does not cut the corpus by a third.

Start with the planner

Try the goal planner (the app's own planner, no sign-in to plan), the retirement corpus calculator, or the emergency fund calculator. Then sign in to run the same numbers on your real funds.

Frequently asked questions

Can I have many goals at once?

Yes. Retirement, two children's education and an emergency fund on one road is the normal setup. The app prioritises the emergency fund first, then goals by date and priority.

Do I have to assign each fund to a goal?

No. Tick which investments are for goals in general; the app divides them by what each goal needs and how far away it is.

What if I am not on track?

The goal shows the gap and the extra SIP that would close it. You can also move the date, change the priority or lower the target.

Does it include EPF and PPF?

For the retirement goal only. They are never part of a sell or switch step.
