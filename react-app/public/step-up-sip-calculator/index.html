How a step-up SIP is calculated

The calculator runs month by month. Every 12 months the SIP amount is multiplied by (1 + step-up %), and each instalment compounds at the monthly rate until the end of the period. The table shows the SIP amount in force each year, the total you have put in, and the value.

Flat SIP vs step-up SIP

₹10,000 a month at 12% for 20 years gives about ₹1 crore. The same SIP stepped up 10% a year gives about ₹1.9 crore, because the later, larger instalments still get 10–15 years of compounding. Your total outlay also rises, so check the table and make sure the year-15 SIP is something your future income can carry.

How to set up a step-up SIP in practice

  1. Decide the step-up as a percentage of your expected yearly raise, not the whole raise. 5–10% is realistic.
  2. Most AMCs and platforms (Zerodha Coin, Groww, Kuvera, MF Central) let you set a top-up SIP once, so it rises automatically.
  3. Track the actual instalments, not the plan. Capital Friends reads every SIP from your CAMS statement and shows the real money-in and XIRR.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good step-up percentage?

Match it to your expected salary growth: 5–10% a year for most salaried investors. A 15% step-up is aggressive and hard to sustain for 20 years.

Is a step-up SIP better than starting with a bigger SIP?

If you can afford the bigger SIP today, start bigger. The step-up is for people whose income will grow; it lets today's SIP stay affordable while the plan still reaches the goal.

Can I step up an existing SIP?

Yes. Either register a new SIP for the extra amount, or use the top-up option your platform offers. Both show up as separate SIP transactions in your CAMS statement.

Does the calculator use annual or monthly compounding?

Monthly. Each instalment compounds at the yearly return divided by 12, which is how SIP calculators on AMFI and AMC sites work.
