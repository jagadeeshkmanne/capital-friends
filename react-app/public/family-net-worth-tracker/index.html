Family Net Worth Tracker

One dashboard for the whole family: every bank account, investment account, mutual fund, stock, FD, EPF, gold, property, loan and insurance policy, tied to the person it belongs to. If something happens to you, your family knows where everything is.

Why families need this, not just investors

In most homes all of the money lives in one person's head. Families that lose that person spend months searching for documents: where are the investments, which bank, who is the nominee. Some of it can be traced with a PAN; much of it cannot. Capital Friends was built so that one email, sent every morning, answers those questions.

What it tracks

Family members

Self, spouse, parents, kids. Everything else belongs to one of them. PAN and Aadhaar are optional, saved only in your sheet and masked on screen.

Bank and investment accounts

A list of which accounts exist and which platform (Zerodha, Groww, a distributor) is linked to which bank. No bank login, ever.

Mutual funds and stocks

Funds from a CAMS statement, stocks from your broker's Excel or a CDSL/NSDL statement, with XIRR and CAGR.

Other investments

FDs, PPF, EPF, NPS, bonds, gold and silver by weight at today's rate, property with its loan linked.

Insurance

Term and health policies with insured member, nominee and sum assured; the app checks whether the cover is enough.

Loans

Home, car and personal loans with EMI, interest and months left; net worth is assets minus loans.

The family email

Every day (or every month) at a time you choose, each member you select gets the Family Financial Blueprint: net worth, every account, every fund and its value, policies and nominees, loans, goals, and which bank account is linked to what. It goes from your own Gmail, to the addresses you enter. There is no Capital Friends server in between.

Privacy by design

Sign in with Google and the app creates one Google Sheet in your Drive; that is the entire database. The developer cannot see it. The mask button hides names and numbers on screen for screen-sharing. The code is open source.

Frequently asked questions

Is the family net worth tracker free?

Yes, completely free and open source, with no ads.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Bank accounts are a list you type, so the family knows which account is linked to what. There is no login, OTP or account aggregator.

Who can see my family's data?

Only your Google account. The data is a Google Sheet in your own Drive; share it only if you choose to.

Can each family member see only their own money?

The app has a "viewing as" switch to look at one member or everyone. The daily email can go to any members you select.

How is gold valued?

By weight and purity at the day's rate, so you never need to update it.
