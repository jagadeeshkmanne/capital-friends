How the SWP calculator works

Every month the corpus earns one month of return and then pays out the withdrawal. If you set an inflation rate, the withdrawal is raised once a year so your income keeps its purchasing power. The table shows the withdrawal in force each year, the total paid out and the corpus left. If the corpus reaches zero before the end, the result tells you how many years it lasted.

The rule of thumb

If the yearly withdrawal is under 4% of the corpus and the corpus earns more than inflation, the money typically lasts 30+ years. ₹1 crore paying ₹33,000 a month is 4%. At ₹50,000 a month (6%) with 6% inflation and an 8% return, the corpus is gone in about 21 years. Try both above.

SWP vs dividend option vs FD interest

  • An SWP lets you decide the amount; a dividend (IDCW) payout is decided by the fund and is fully taxable.
  • Only the gain inside each withdrawal is taxed, which usually makes an SWP more tax-efficient than FD interest.
  • Withdrawing from equity in a crash locks in losses. A bucket approach, where 2–3 years of expenses sit in safe funds, avoids that. See the three-bucket retirement strategy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a safe SWP rate?

Around 3.5–4% of the corpus a year, rising with inflation, has historically lasted 30 years in most cases. Higher rates work only if returns stay high, which you cannot count on.

Is SWP taxable?

Each withdrawal is part capital and part gain; only the gain portion is taxed, at the capital-gains rate for that fund type. That is why SWPs are usually better than taking dividends or FD interest in a high tax bracket.

Which fund is good for SWP?

A low-volatility fund such as a conservative hybrid, balanced advantage or short-duration debt fund for the income part, with equity kept separate for growth. Capital Friends models this as three buckets.

What happens to an SWP in a market fall?

More units are sold to pay the same amount, which hurts long-term value. Keeping two years of withdrawals in a safe bucket and pausing equity withdrawals during a fall solves this.
