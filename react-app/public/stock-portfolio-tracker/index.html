Stock Portfolio Tracker

Keep every family member's stocks beside their mutual funds, FDs and gold, so the dashboard shows the real total. Import holdings from your broker's Excel file or a CDSL/NSDL statement; prices update daily.

How stocks come in

  1. Download the holdings file from your broker (Zerodha Console, Groww, Upstox, ICICI Direct and others export an Excel or CSV), or your CDSL/NSDL consolidated statement.
  2. Create a stock portfolio for the family member and import the file. Each holding gets its quantity and average price.
  3. Prices refresh daily. You see current value, profit, allocation across the family, and the stocks in your top and bottom performers.

Stocks and funds together

Most trackers do one or the other. Capital Friends shows stocks and mutual funds in the same net worth, the same family email and the same goals, so a family with a Zerodha account, an SIP portfolio, an EPF and a house sees one honest number.

Track the real thing, not just the estimate

Capital Friends is a free, open-source portfolio tracker for Indian families. Import your CAMS statement, see XIRR and CAGR for every fund, plan goals and retirement, and let your family get one summary email. Your data stays in your own Google Drive.

Frequently asked questions

Which brokers are supported?

Any broker that exports holdings as Excel or CSV with symbol, quantity and average price. CDSL and NSDL statements also work.

Are stock prices live?

They update daily through a public price sheet; this is a tracker for long-term holdings, not a trading terminal.

Can I track my spouse's and parents' demat accounts?

Yes. Add each person as a family member, create their stock portfolio and import their holdings file.

Is there buy/sell advice?

No. The app shows facts: value, profit, allocation and distance from the high. Decisions are yours.
