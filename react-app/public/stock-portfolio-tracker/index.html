Stock portfolio tracker

Direct stocks sit in the same family picture as mutual funds: import your holdings from your broker's Excel or a depository statement, see the value and profit of every stock for every member, and let the dashboard, net worth and goals count them. No broker login, nothing automated against your demat account.

Why stocks belong next to the funds

A family that holds ₹20 lakh of stocks in two demat accounts and ₹2 crore of mutual funds does not have two problems; it has one net worth and one set of goals. Keeping stocks in a separate app means the family email, the goal planner and the retirement buckets all miss them. Here the stock holdings are one more tab under the investment account they live in.

How to import

  1. Download your holdings: in Zerodha Console, Groww, Upstox or any broker there is an export to Excel/CSV; or download the monthly CDSL or NSDL consolidated statement that covers every demat account.
  2. Open Stocks → Import, pick the member and the investment account, and upload the file. A sample Excel with the expected columns (symbol, quantity, average price) is linked on the page.
  3. Check the preview (symbol, quantity, average cost) and confirm. Holdings for the same account are updated, not duplicated.
  4. Add a few shares by hand with Add stock when you buy between imports.

What you see

Per stock

Quantity, average price, current price, invested, current value, profit and percentage.

Per member and per account

Which demat account holds what, mapped to the bank it pays into.

In the family total

Stocks count in the dashboard's asset allocation, the net worth, and the daily family email.

In goals

Stocks can be included in the money for goals like any other investment.

Prices

Stock prices are refreshed with the daily update in your own Google account, alongside mutual fund NAVs and the gold rate, so the value on the dashboard and in the morning email is current.

What it is not

It is a holdings tracker, not a trading app. There is no order placement, no intraday feed and no broker API. For traders that is a limitation; for a family record, it is the point: nothing in the app can move your money.

Track the real thing, not just the estimate

Capital Friends is a free, open-source portfolio tracker for Indian families. Import your CAMS statement, see XIRR and CAGR for every fund, plan goals and retirement, and let your family get one summary email. Your data stays in your own Google Drive.

Frequently asked questions

Which brokers are supported?

Any broker that exports holdings to Excel or CSV (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One, ICICI Direct and others), plus CDSL and NSDL consolidated statements that cover every demat account.

Does it connect to my demat account?

No. You upload a file; the app never logs in to a broker or depository.

Are stock prices live?

They are refreshed daily by the update that runs in your Google account, which is enough for a family net worth. It is not an intraday terminal.

Can I track ESOPs or unlisted shares?

Add them under Other Investments with a value you update yourself.

Will the family email show my stocks?

Yes, each member's stock holdings and their value appear in that member's section of the daily email.
