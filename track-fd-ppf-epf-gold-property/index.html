FDs, PPF, EPF, gold, property and loans

For most families the biggest numbers are not mutual funds: the house, the home loan, EPF after twenty years, the gold in the locker. Capital Friends tracks all of it under Other Investments and Liabilities, revalues gold every day, links a loan to the property it bought, and uses EPF and PPF correctly in the retirement plan.

Gold by weight

You do not need to know what your gold is worth. Enter the weight, say 250 grams, and the purity, 22 carat. The app looks up today's rate, shows the estimated value, and revalues it every day with the daily update. The live 24K and 22K gold and silver rates are shown at the top of the page.

The house and its loan

  1. Add Investment → type Real Estate; a name and whose it is.
  2. What you paid (say ₹1.25 Cr) and roughly what it is worth today (₹1.6 Cr).
  3. Under Linked loans, click Create new loan and enter the lender, outstanding balance, EMI and interest rate, right in the same form.
  4. Add. The house appears in the assets with its loan linked, the loan appears under Liabilities with the EMI, rate and months left, and net worth is assets minus loans.

Liabilities

Home loan, car loan, personal loan, a loan from a relative: each with the lender, outstanding amount, EMI, interest rate and the number of months left, computed from the EMI. The dashboard shows total assets, total loans and net worth separately, and the daily email tells the family exactly what is owed and to whom; the loan nobody knew about is a common and expensive surprise.

EPF, PPF and NPS in retirement

These are retirement money with their own rules, so the goal planner treats them accordingly: they count towards the retirement goal only, they are never part of a switch or sell step in the bucket plan, and when one is paid out you press Received and tell the app which liquid or debt fund the money went to.

On the dashboard

After adding an FD, EPF and gold the dashboard shows the split the first time most people see it: for example mutual funds 73% of the family's money, gold 12%, EPF 11%, FD 4%. Many families discover how much is in one place only here.

Frequently asked questions

How is gold valued?

By weight and purity at the live market rate, refreshed daily. Enter 250 g of 22K and the app does the rest; you can override with a value if you prefer.

Can I link a loan to the property it bought?

Yes. Create the loan inside the property form under Linked loans. The property shows its loan and the loan shows its asset.

How does EPF count in retirement?

As retirement money only: it is included in the retirement goal and the stability bucket but never suggested for a sell or switch, since it has its own withdrawal rules.

Do I have to update FD and PPF values?

FDs can be set with a maturity date and the app reminds you; PPF, EPF and NPS you update when the statement arrives. Gold and funds update themselves.

Does the family email include loans?

Yes: lender, outstanding, EMI and months left, for every loan, in every email.
