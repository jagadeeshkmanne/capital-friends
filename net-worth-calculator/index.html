How it works, in one picture

Why net worth, not just investments

A ₹50 lakh portfolio with a ₹60 lakh loan is a negative number. Tracking only investments hides that. Net worth also shows how much is liquid: money in funds, FDs and gold can pay for an emergency; the house you live in cannot.

What to include

  • Assets: mutual funds and stocks at today's value; FDs, PPF, bonds; EPF and NPS balances; gold by weight at today's rate; property at a conservative market value; bank balances.
  • Liabilities: outstanding principal of every loan, and any credit card balance you carry.
  • Leave out: the car's resale value (optional), insurance cover (it is protection, not wealth), and money owed to you that you may not get back.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include my house in net worth?

Yes, at a conservative value, but look at liquid net worth separately. Many people are house-rich and cash-poor.

How often should I calculate net worth?

Once a quarter is enough if you do it by hand. A tracker that updates fund prices daily makes it effortless and lets you see the trend.

What is a good net worth by age in India?

A rough guide is 1× yearly income by 30, 3× by 40, 6× by 50 and 10× by 60, excluding the home you live in. Your own trend matters more than the benchmark.

How do I value gold?

Weight in grams × today's rate for its purity (24K, 22K or 18K). Capital Friends values physical gold this way every day.
